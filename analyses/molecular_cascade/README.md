# Molecular cascade analysis

This extension verifies frozen transfer results against state-compatible regulatory evidence. Rules, resources, thresholds, exclusions, and denominators are fixed in `config/molecular_cascade.yaml` and recorded by SHA-256 in `freeze_manifest.json` before regulatory annotations are joined to the existing outcomes.

The extension is retrospective because the primary transfer results already existed. It is not described as a prospective preregistration. Overlapping TF databases are treated as corroborating provenance, not independent observations. Direct-regulation claims require the complete Tier 1 gate. Missing occupancy, guide-resolved vectors, and donor-by-guide effects remain unavailable rather than being imputed.

Run `python analyses/molecular_cascade/run_analysis.py`, then `python figures/fig08/make_figure.py`. An isolated worktree can read the frozen inputs through `REGULATORY_SOURCE_ROOT`; all outputs remain in the current worktree. Input hashes are checked before analysis. Existing validated primary fits are reused.

The source response matrix contains significant log2 fold changes divided by `max(abs(cis effect), 0.1)`. An absent entry means no retained significant response, not a measured zero. Weighted-mean and centred univariate-regression activity scores therefore remain response-programme proxies. The frozen A-B regulon sensitivity has the same eligible edges as the A-only network and does not provide an expanded confidence-level test.

The implementation audit corrected BED coordinate conversion, endpoint liftover checks, matching-caliper enforcement, sign-dependent path selection, the regression activity statistic, validation leakage in molecular tiers, and fold-external imputation. Corrections are recorded in `results/extension_rules.json`. The historical freeze is unchanged. The legacy column `direct_support_fraction` is a regulatory-support proxy including motifs and curated edges; it must never be described as directly occupied regulation. Guide and K562 outcomes do not contribute to the molecular evidence count or held-out predictive features. Alternative secondary intermediates and conflicting signs are retained.

The complete denominator is 284 eligible regulators, 715 regulator-state rows and 124,798 response edges. No edge meets Tier 1. There are 36 Tier 2, 2,490 Tier 3 and 122,272 Tier 4 edges. The compatible CTCF occupancy resource has no eligible matching perturbation. Consequently, direct-edge timing and direct-regulation claims fail the availability gate.

The GATA3-EGR1 hypothesis remains unsupported. EGR1 is not retained in the significant GATA3 response vector in any state. None of the six matched EGR1 motif or curated-enrichment comparisons survives correction. EGR1 perturbation has six retained genes at 8 hours and two at 48 hours, below the 20-gene independent-programme gate. SP3, USF1, HIF1A, CREB1 and CEBPB are the fixed degree-matched negative factors. The complete exploratory screen contains 126,811 source-state-secondary-resource comparisons, including 22 corrected motif enrichments and 101 corrected curated enrichments; enrichment alone is not a mechanistic result.

Regulatory support is associated with transfer gain, guide concordance and K562 vector concordance in clustered analyses. Its association with rerouting is opposite to the proposed direction. All 18 matched state-change tests remain unresolved. The natural-genetic tier comparison contains only 12 independent targets and is underpowered. Disease-programme convergence remains unresolved.

After excluding validation leakage, added molecular features change held-out guide R-squared by +0.043 (95% interval -0.020 to 0.101) and K562 R-squared by -0.422 (-1.415 to 0.031). Both comparisons remain unresolved. These models use already inspected target-matched datasets, not a new untouched system. Empirical training-residual intervals are reported with observed coverage and are not described as conformal intervals.

Matched controls cover 73,210 of the 124,798 edges. Every unmatched edge remains in the evidence matrix. Conditional matched-gene permutations preserve the frozen case-control sets; their central ranges are null-reference intervals. The degree-preserving null uses the frozen 250 realizations with one successful swap per edge and is reported with that mixing limitation. Database-component removal is an ablation, not an independent leave-one-study-out replication. State-specific occupancy, matched chromatin changes, guide-resolved programmes, donor-by-guide effects and unsupported causal links are retained in the unavailable-estimand tables.

Verification combines exact source-row checks, independent regression-statistic calculation, deterministic edge and path checks, target-held-out split checks, complete multiple-testing families, and PNG/PDF/SVG inspection. Figure 8 uses exact source tables and editable vector text. No primary values or fitted primary models are changed.
